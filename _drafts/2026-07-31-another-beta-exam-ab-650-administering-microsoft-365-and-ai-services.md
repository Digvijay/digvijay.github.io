I recently had the chance to take another beta exam from Microsoft: **AB-650: Administering Microsoft 365 and AI Services**.

Taking beta exams is always an interesting experience. There are no official practice tests, community study guides are sparse, and you have to rely heavily on official documentation and hands-on experience with the platform.

In this post, I want to share my thoughts on the exam structure, key focus areas, and how to prepare if you are planning to take it once it officially releases.

---

## Key Exam Domains & Focus Areas

1. **Microsoft 365 Copilot & AI Governance**:
   - Data Protection & Data Loss Prevention (DLP) for AI prompts.
   - Managing tenant-level AI permissions and sensitivity labels.

2. **Azure AI & Microsoft Foundry Integrations**:
   - Connecting M365 tenant services with enterprise AI agents.
   - Identity, RBAC, and Audit Logging for AI workflows.

3. **Administration & Monitoring**:
   - Monitoring AI usage, response latency, and security compliance.
   - Administering tenant settings via PowerShell and Microsoft Graph API.
